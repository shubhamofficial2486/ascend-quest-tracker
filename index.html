About the Stats

STR — Strength / Fitness   INT — Intellect / Study & Learning   VIT — Vitality / Health & Habits   AGI — Agility / Skill Practice   PER — Perception / Creativity & Focus

Each Main Quest is tagged with one stat. Completing its daily tasks feeds EXP into that stat — and into your overall Hunter Level.

Danger Zone

Wipes every Main Quest, weekly plan, task, and logged Special Quest, and resets your Hunter profile back to Level 1 / Rank E / 0 EXP. This cannot be undone.
